ABOUT THE PROJECT

Making medical AI easier to understand.

BrainAI is an educational and research prototype demonstrating how deep learning can be applied to brain MRI image classification.

PROJECT PURPOSE

What is BrainAI?

BrainAI demonstrates an end-to-end computer vision workflow starting from MRI image upload and preprocessing through model inference and visual explanation.

The system was developed as a portfolio, learning and research project to demonstrate machine learning, deep learning, Flask deployment and explainable AI.

04 Explain

GRAD-CAM

How explainability works

Grad-CAM, or Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping, uses gradients from the model to identify image regions that contributed to a selected prediction.

In BrainAI, the visualization is generated after prediction and displayed alongside the model's classification result.

Important

A Grad-CAM heatmap is an explanation aid, not proof that a particular anatomical region contains a disease.

RESEARCH & EDUCATIONAL PROTOTYPE

Not a clinical diagnostic system.

BrainAI is intended for research, education and experimentation. Its predictions should not replace professional medical evaluation.
